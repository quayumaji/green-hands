A family-kitchen welcome

Food with roots.
Made for sharing.

Green Hands African Restaurant was built around a simple idea: put honest West and East African food on the table, cooked from scratch with real spices and a family-kitchen welcome.

Our menu brings together comforting rice plates, soulful soups and swallow, and familiar favorites made our way. We honor the ingredients and cooking traditions that inspire us, while making every guest feel at home in Glen Burnie.

We believe a good meal should feel generous—in flavor, in care, and in the people gathered around it. Come by, take a plate to go, and make yourself part of our table.
